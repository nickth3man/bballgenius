## Phase 1: Internal Logic & Cross-Reconciliation

* **The Aggregate Continuity Check:** For a subset of random players across different eras, write a script to sum up their game logs (`fact_player_game_boxscore`) for a given season and compare it to their row in the season totals table (`fact_player_season_stats`). They must align perfectly.

## Phase 2: Automated Advanced Stat Recalculation

Advanced metrics like True Shooting Percentage ($TS\%$), Effective Field Goal Percentage ($eFG\%$), and Usage Rate ($USG\%$) are highly prone to calculation drift if a null value or missing team-possession data messes up the formula.

* **Deterministic Testing:** Do not trust the imported advanced stat columns blindly. Write a Python script using the DuckDB API to manually calculate $TS\%$ and $eFG\%$ directly from the raw points, field goal attempts, and free throw attempts:

$$TS\% = \frac{\text{Points}}{2 \times (\text{FGA} + 0.44 \times \text{FTA})}$$


* **The Discrepancy Log:** Run this calculation for all 1.66M+ player game rows and flag any row where your computed metric deviates from the stored metric by more than a rounding error (e.g., $> 0.001$). Populated errors go directly into your `audit` schema.

## Phase 3: The "Historical Edge Case" Boundary Scan

NBA history is full of weird statistical anomalies and rule updates. You can write simple SQL constraint checks to catch corrupt historical data.

### Bio Data Logical Constraints

* **Age/Draft Sanity:** Ensure `draft_year` minus `birth_date` year is always between 17 and 30. Anyone drafted at age 8 or age 50 is a corrupted row.
* **Height & Weight Bounds:** Query for any player where height is outside 5'3" to 7'7", or weight is outside 130 lbs to 375 lbs. Catch corrupt zero-values or nulls masquerading as data.
* **Alternative Academies:** Check your `school` or pre-NBA columns for modern pathways. Ensure players like Jalen Green or Scoot Henderson properly list "G League Ignite," and international players list their respective overseas clubs rather than being left blank or marked "Undrafted."

### Historical Rule Violations

* **The 3-Point Line (1979-80):** Query your database for any games played prior to the 1979-80 season where `fg3a` (Three-point attempts) or `fg3m` is greater than 0. If there are any, your historical data ingestion is leaking modern stats into the past.
* **Tracking Stats (1973-74):** Blocks (`blk`) and steals (`stl`) were not officially tracked until 1973-74. Ensure all rows prior to 1973 are either strictly null or safely handled so your application doesn't accidentally treat historical legends like Bill Russell as having "0 blocks" per game.
